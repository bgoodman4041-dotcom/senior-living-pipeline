# Operator Partners: Who ATCO Should Build With

Prepared 2026-11-03 for Ben. Companion to `operator-benchmarks.md` and `riverspring-model.md`. Facts are sourced and linked; anything marked **(inferred)** is our judgment and needs a call to confirm.

---

## 1. Bottom line

1. **Lead rental AL/MC partner: Benchmark Senior Living with National Development.** They have the deepest footprint in our geography (68 Northeast communities, 21 in CT, 9 in Fairfield County), an active development JV doing exactly our product (80–90 unit AL/MC on suburban pads in NJ and NY), and Benchmark is **already the named operator on our Cortlandt site** (Evergreen Manor, Feb 2026 Planning Board).
2. **NJ pads inside age-restricted master plans: Engel Burman (The Bristal).** Their "look-alike CCRC" (age-restricted IL plus an adjacent AL/MC building) is the same configuration as Florham Park and Montville. They run 23 Bristal communities across Long Island, Westchester, Manhattan and North Jersey at mid-90s occupancy, backed by Harrison Street.
3. **Luxury Westchester and Rye Brook: Sunrise.** BDT & MSD Partners is buying a majority stake (about $1B, announced Oct 6, 2026). Sunrise has a 50+ community, roughly $7.5B pipeline, plans 6–7 starts next year, and names Tarrytown NY as a target. It is already the contract vendee at 900 King Street, Rye Brook.
4. **Nonprofit and entrance-fee track: LCS as developer-manager, with a local nonprofit sponsor.** LCS built Broadview at Purchase College, the closest template we have to RiverSpring for a for-profit partner. For Rye Brook, the natural sponsor to approach is **The Osborn** in Rye (inferred): a nonprofit CCRC on 55.8 acres next door, whose own on-campus expansion stalled on neighbor opposition.
5. **Two red flags for the NJ shortlist.** Arbor Terrace Florham Park (80 AL/MC units, Braemar and GEM Realty JV) announced construction on Oct 13, 2026, opening late 2027, in the same submarket as our Florham Park and Whippany pads. Both pads need absorption re-underwritten before we pitch anyone.

## 4. Deal structures to offer

| Structure | Who uses it | ATCO economics | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Management contract on an ATCO-owned asset** | Sunrise, Atria, Juniper, Benchmark | Keep 90–100% of equity; pay roughly 5% of revenue plus an incentive fee **(inferred, industry norm)** | Where ATCO wants the long-term hold and lender-approved operator |
| **Development JV with operator affiliate** | Benchmark + National Development; Arbor + Braemar/GEM | ATCO as developer/GC and co-GP; operator co-invests a small slice **(inferred: 5–10%)**; institutional LP equity | The default for the NJ and NY pads. Lenders financing Benchmark at East Islip show the model is bankable in 2026 |
| **Sale of an entitled pad, or ground lease** | Brightview, Engel Burman, Sunrise | Land profit plus GC contract where negotiable | Where the operator insists on control (Brightview and Engel Burman self-build) |
| **Fee development for a nonprofit sponsor** | LCS + Purchase College; ID2 at River's Edge | Development fee of about 4–5% of cost, plus GC and possibly a ground rent | Rye Brook and any institutional land. Tax-exempt bonds need a 501(c)(3) borrower |
| **Triple-net lease to an operator** | Now rare for new builds; REITs prefer RIDEA management structures | Fixed rent, but the strong operators won't sign | Don't lead with it |

## 5. Next steps (suggested order)

1. **Benchmark / National Development:** pitch Whippany and Poughkeepsie as a two-site program; ask about their stance on Cortlandt and whether ATCO can come in as capital or GC.
2. **Engel Burman:** pitch Florham Park and Montville as look-alike CCRC pads with the age-restricted builders already approved.
3. **Sunrise:** open a conversation on Rye Brook (GC or fee-development role) and their Westchester pipeline, given the new BDT & MSD capital.
4. **LCS + The Osborn:** test appetite for a nonprofit entrance-fee version of Rye Brook, as the fallback if Sunrise doesn't close.
5. **Epoch:** memory-care component at Trumbull.
6. Before any LOI in Morris County, re-run absorption with Arbor Terrace Florham Park in the supply set.

## 6. Market backdrop

- NIC MAP Q3 2026: occupancy across the 31 primary markets was **90.4%** (stabilized 90.8%), up 180 bps year over year, the 21st straight quarterly gain and the first time above 90% since before 2008. New supply has stalled. That is why operators are hungry for well-located, entitled sites, and why ATCO's entitled pads are the leverage in every conversation.
