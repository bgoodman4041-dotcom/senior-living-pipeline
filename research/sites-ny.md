# New York State Site Sourcing: Senior Living (RiverSpring-style)

Prepared 2026-10-19. Area covered: NY within about a 2-hour drive of Midtown. Hurdle: 6.5% stabilized YOC.
Companion CSV: `/mnt/project-files/senior-living/research/sites-ny.csv` (18 rows: 16 candidates plus 2 comps/partners).

**How to read this:** Facts marked **[V]** come straight from the cited source. Facts marked **[I]** are inferred: drive times, lat/lon, utilities and status where the source is silent. Every lat/lon is approximate. Asking prices are given only where a source published one.

**Research limits:** The session's web-search budget (200 calls) ran out during research. Two Westfair pages returned rate limits: "Legionaries selling Westchester properties" and "Plans revealed for Ridgeway Country Club property". Because of that, the following were **not** gathered:
- town median home values
- 75+ household counts
- a fuller Orange/Dutchess/Putnam/Nassau sweep

These are the next diligence steps (see the end).

## Tier 1: strongest acquisition / JV targets

### NY-01: 900 King Street, Rye Brook (Westchester), 17.3 ac. Approved but unbuilt
- **Entitlement [V]:** The village rezoned the site and approved the site plan for an age-restricted campus of 231 units: 126 IL, 85 AL and 20 townhomes, 14 of them affordable. The zoning petition was filed June 2017 and approval took about 5 years. The plan was cut from 269 units.
- **Status [V]:** The village project page reads "Awaiting Construction". The owner is Rye King Associates LLC / 900 King Street Owner LLC (George Comfort & Sons). The site is a largely vacant office building with parking.
- **Price:** not public. Utilities: municipal [I]. Drive: about 45 minutes [I].
- **Approval climate [V]:** Rye Brook has now accepted Erickson's 834-unit senior PUD for review (see comps). Opposition risk is medium-low because the site is already entitled.
- **Why it matters:** This is the closest thing to a shovel-ready IL/AL/MC campus that fits the 150-220 unit prototype. The approach is to buy the entitlement from the owner or JV with it.
- **Sources:**
  - https://ryebrookny.gov/900-king-street
  - https://westfaironline.com/145622/groundbreaking-expected-for-rye-brook-senior-project
  - https://westfaironline.com/94495/senior-housing-planned-for-rye-brook-office-building-campus/

### NY-02: 236 Grasslands Rd, Valhalla (Town of Mount Pleasant, Westchester), about 8 ac. Floating zone granted
- **Entitlement [V]:** In March 2021 the Town Board voted unanimously to apply a new "Service Enriched Senior Housing" floating zone to the site for Brightview's 170 units (89 IL, 45 AL, 36 MC). The zone is open to sites of 8+ acres with frontage on a state or county road. Site plan review went to the Planning Board.
- **Status:** I found no operating Brightview in Valhalla, so the project was probably never built [I]. A LoopNet broker flyer (Academic Co-Realty) marks the land "Under Contract", date unknown [V]. Zoning is R-20, and the flyer says nursing homes and AL are allowed by right or special permit.
- **Utilities:** Brightview committed about $1M to a water main and district extension [V]. Drive: about 40 minutes [I].
- **Risk [V]:** traffic on Grasslands Rd and EMS load. Some residents opposed.
- **Sources:**
  - https://planning.westchestercountyny.gov/images/archive/stories/PlanningBoard/Referrals/2021Letters/mtp21002.pdf
  - https://www.theexaminernews.com/mount-pleasant-approves-floating-zone-for-valhalla-senior-housing-project
  - https://images.loopnet.ca/d2/4ZFLsQequU-oZyqDNSNFXYky-72kj_MdCtbUE7ZABI8/236grasslandsflyer.pdf

### NY-03 and NY-04: Cortlandt Medical Oriented District (MOD), across from NYP Hudson Valley Hospital, Westchester
- **Zoning [V]:** The Town Board unanimously created the MOD in 2023 to encourage AL, IL and SNF next to the hospital. It grew out of the 2016 *Envision Cortlandt* comprehensive plan. Strong support from town policy.
- **NY-03 Evergreen Manor, 2003 Crompond Rd [V]:**
  - About 28 ac (17 + 11), with municipal sewer and water.
  - VS Construction (Santucci) proposes 100-120 AL/MC units, 99 townhomes and commercial space.
  - The Planning Board hearing was adjourned to June 2, 2026 over stormwater, fire access and a retaining wall. DOT traffic work must be done before final approval.
  - Approach: JV, or buy the AL pad.
- **NY-04 Gyrodyne Cortlandt Medical Park [V]:**
  - 14 ac, of which 9.79 are developable. It holds 31,420 sf of medical office space.
  - **Listed for sale by JLL** as part of Gyrodyne's liquidation. Price not public.
  - It is MOD-eligible for AL/IL/SNF and is a natural fit for a health-system partnership.
- **Drive:** about 60 minutes [I].
- **Sources:**
  - https://peekskillherald.com/24305/news/coming-soon-to-cortlandt-a-big-box-store-assisted-living-and-plenty-more
  - https://www.townofcortlandtny.gov/documents/mod/evergreen_eaf_project_narrative_revised.pdf
  - https://www.theexaminernews.com/?p=75353172

### NY-05: Flowerfield, St. James (Town of Smithtown, Suffolk), 49 ac vacant. Under contract
- **Deal [V]:** Gyrodyne signed in August 2025 to sell to B2K Smithtown LLC for **$24.0M-$28.74M**, with assisted living as the intended use. Gyrodyne's earlier plan was a 250-unit AL, a hotel and medical office.
- **Approvals [V]:** An 8-lot subdivision was approved in 2022. The court upheld the town, but an appeal is pending.
- **Remainder:** Gyrodyne's other 14 ac, which hold buildings, are still being marketed by JLL.
- **Risk:** HIGH. The local preservation coalition is litigating, and neighbors fought the nearby 97-bed Whisper Mills AL in 2023.
- **Approach:** a fallback if B2K's deal fails, or a JV with B2K. Drive: about 85 minutes [I].
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.globenewswire.com/fr/news-release/2025/08/04/3126773/0/en/GYRODYNE-LLC-ANNOUNCES-AGREEMENT-TO-SELL-49-ACRE-PARCEL-IN-SMITHTOWN-NEW-YORK-AS-PART-OF-STRATEGIC-LIQUIDATION-PLAN.html
  - https://tbrnewsmedia.com/tag/sale-of-flowerfield/
  - https://tbrnewsmedia.com/?p=154545

### NY-06: Harrison Avenue Retirement Community, Riverhead (Suffolk), 47 ac
- **Application [V]:** A straight rezoning from Hamlet Residence to the town's **Retirement Community** district. The plan is 81 AL/MC units on about 10 ac and 60 IL units on about 37 ac. Filed December 30 (2025) by Harrison Avenue LLC.
- **Opposition:** none reported. Riverhead's board split 3-2 on the 2013 Concordia CCRC.
- **Fit:** Acreage suits a phased life-plan campus. Drive is about 2 hours, so it is at the edge of the range [I].
- **Sources:**
  - https://riverheadlocal.com/2026/08/21/riverhead-assisted-living-zone-change/
  - https://patch.com/new-york/riverhead/board-divided-over-assisted-living-proposal

### NY-07: Former Woodmere Club, northern 34 ac (Town of Hempstead, Nassau)
- **Application [V]:** Developers Weiss and Gerszberg cut a plan for about 300 houses down to over-55 luxury condos. The Hempstead Town Board has held a hearing but scheduled no vote.
- **Climate [V]:** The Woodsburgh mayor supports the senior plan, but some residents remain opposed. Hempstead took about 9 years to entitle Sunrise's 84-unit AL in Oceanside, which closed in 2026.
- **Approach:** JV to add AL/MC to the program. Drive: about 50 minutes [I].
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.yahoo.com/news/controversial-push-develop-former-tony-195217653.html
  - https://www.lee-associates.com/new-york/2026/07/09/sunrise-senior-living-oceanside-land-acquisition/

### NY-08: Former IBM / Legion of Christ Thornwood center, 582 Columbus Ave (Mount Pleasant)
- **History [V]:** The site is about 262-265 ac. The Legion offered it for sale in 2012-13, and EF Academy bought 97 ac and two buildings in 2014.
- **Unknowns:** Who owns the roughly 167-acre remainder now is **unverified**.
- **Path:** If Columbus Ave counts as a county road, the remainder could qualify for Mount Pleasant's senior floating zone [I]. This is the only life-plan-scale (20-40+ ac) Westchester lead found.
- **Next step:** a title search.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.bishop-accountability.org/2014/05/ef-academy-to-add-thornwood-campus/
  - https://www.theexaminernews.com/changes-may-be-on-the-way-for-thornwood-legion-of-christ-property

## Tier 2: opportunistic, longer-dated, or edge of range

- Zoned Office Park, which does not allow residential.
- An Atlantic Realty group (bought 2024) wants a zoning amendment for 342 townhomes and condos, and a full EIS is required.
- Pitch: add a senior or care component to help the rezoning [I].
- Source: https://www.hgar.com/major-redevelopment-proposal-could-bring-342-housing-units-to-former-ibm-campus-in-rockland-county

**NY-10: Hudson Heritage, Town of Poughkeepsie (Dutchess), 156 ac [V]**
- Approved 750-unit intergenerational plan that includes AL. Under construction by EFG/Saber.
- Approach: pad or operator JV. Drive: about 1h45 [I].
- Source: https://realestateindepth.com/work-progresses-on-300m-hudson-heritage-mixed-use-project-in-town-of-poughkeepsie

**NY-11: Blue Rill, Airmont (Rockland), about 18 ac [V]**
- The RSH senior floating zone applies by special permit, and the FEIS was accepted in 2025.
- Rockland County Planning **disapproved** it as overdevelopment, citing flood zone and wetlands.
- The owner paid $8M in 2021.
- Source: https://ricktannenbaum.substack.com/p/plans-advance-for-senior-housing

**NY-12: Hamptonburgh (Orange), Maybrook Rd, 88.66 ac [V]**
- R-4A, where age-restricted housing is allowed by Town Board special permit. The current plan is only 72 active-adult units.
- Base case uses septic and wells, with an alternative of Maybrook water. Infrastructure limits AL/IL density.
- Source: https://dec.ny.gov/news/environmental-notice-bulletin/2025-07-09/seqr/town-of-hamptonburgh-all-mine-of-orange-inc-active-adult-community

**NY-13: Landmark Colony / Farm Colony, Staten Island, 45 ac [V]**

**NY-14: Letchworth Village / Patriot Hills (Stony Point & Haverstraw) [V]**
