# New Jersey Site Sourcing: Senior Living Campus (RiverSpring-style)

Prepared 2026-10-19. Geography: NJ within about a 2-hour drive of Midtown Manhattan.
Prototype: 8-15 ac rental IL/AL/MC (150-220 units) or 20-40+ ac life-plan/continuum (250-400 units). Hurdle: 6.5% stabilized YOC.

**How to read this.** Each fact is tagged **[V]** (Verified: stated in the cited source) or **[I]** (Inferred: my estimate or judgment, which needs checking). Drive times are my off-peak estimates to Midtown, not measured. Lat/lon are approximate, to within about 0.5 mile.

**Limits of this research.** The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. No live LoopNet or Crexi listing pages could be read (Crexi pages returned no listing content). As a result:
- Most candidates below are **entitled or redevelopment-designated sites already held by someone else**. You would get them by acquisition, JV, ground lease or an unsolicited approach, not by bidding on a listing.
- None of the sites has a confirmed public asking price for the land. The only prices found were closed trades: Paramus at $9.25M and Montvale (Western Union HQ) at $5.5M.
- Median home values and 75+ household counts were **not** pulled for most towns. Pull ESRI or Census ACS data before underwriting.

A follow-up session could fill in: live broker listings (LoopNet, Crexi, C&W, JLL); the current status of Hopewell Scotch Road, Wilson Farm and Fort Monmouth Parcel B; and the Thrive at Montvale site.

## 1. Regulatory screen (NJ-specific)

**Assisted living CN.** A new AL residence in NJ needs a DOH Certificate of Need **and** a license [V]. In practice, recent approvals have gone through **Expedited Review (N.J.A.C. 8:33-5)** [V]. Examples:
- Peapack Senior Living: ERCN filed 2025-07-31, approved 2026-07-10. That is roughly a **12-month CN timeline** [V/I].
- Arbor Terrace Florham Park: ERCN approved 2024-05-21 [V].

Sources: https://www.nj.gov/health/healthfacilities/documents/CN/2026/ercn-peapack-senior-living.pdf ; https://www.nj.gov/health/healthfacilities/certificate-need/expedited.shtml

**Medicaid set-aside (affects YOC).** Standard CN condition:
- A new AL or comprehensive personal care home "shall have a **Medicaid occupancy level of 10 percent within three years of licensure**".
- That level must then be "continuously maintained" [V].
- Source: https://nj.gov/health/healthfacilities/documents/CN/2024/ercn-arbor-terrance-florham-park-05212024.pdf

Underwrite 10% of AL beds at NJ Medicaid MLTSS rates. These beds can **also be counted as Mount Laurel affordable credits**. Montgomery's plan requires 20 Medicaid-eligible AL beds as its affordable component [V] (https://www.montgomerynj.gov/DocumentCenter/View/765/Country-Club-Meadows-Senior-Living-Redevelopment-Plan---March-20-2025-PDF). Harrington Park counts 9 Allegro Medicaid AL beds as 4th-round credits [V] (https://harringtonparknj.gov/government/documents/forms-documents/department-documents/planning-board/937-fair-share-housing-plan-amendment-to-the-fourth-round-housing-element-fair-share-plan-for-the-borough-of-harrington-park-february-2026/file).

**Skilled nursing beds.** New SNF beds are tightly controlled by CN. Plan to **buy or transfer existing licensed beds**, or to use CCRC resident-only bed provisions [I: confirm with NJ DOH / CN counsel]. Example: Tryko is building a new 180-bed SNF in Montvale (2026) [V] (https://hoodline.com/2026/09/old-western-union-hq-in-montvale-becomes-180-bed-nursing-home/), which shows bed rights can be assembled. For a RiverSpring-style campus, phase SNF last and source beds by acquisition [I].

**Proposed rule change.** A Jan 2026 DOH proposal would extend "deemed status" (accreditation in place of state inspection) to CPCHs. ALRs already qualify. It makes **no CN change** [V] (https://nj.gov/health/legal/documents/notice-of-rule-proposal/8_36-1.3_2.4%20NOP.pdf).

**Mount Laurel 4th round (2025-2035).** Towns adopted fair-share plans in mid-2025. Examples:
- Florham Park's 4th-round prospective need is 305 units [V] (https://njbiz.com/garden-homes-florham-park-55-acre-305-units/).
- Age-restricted inclusionary projects and Medicaid AL beds are being used as compliance tools in Montgomery, Florham Park and Harrington Park [V, sources above].

This is the single best lever for town receptivity. Offer Medicaid AL beds plus age-restricted affordable IL units as credits [I].

**Highlands and Pinelands.**
- Morris, Sussex, Warren, Hunterdon and parts of Passaic/Somerset fall in the Highlands Region. Preservation Area parcels are effectively undevelopable. For example, the 35-ac Goodland Country Club in Mount Olive ($2.9M ask) is described as unable to be redeveloped under Highlands rules [V] (https://bronx.news12.com/former-site-of-mt-olive-nudist-resort-up-for-sale).
- Prefer previously developed corporate campuses with existing sewer. Check each Morris/Somerset parcel against the Highlands Council map [I].
- Pinelands affects southern Ocean and Burlington counties only. None of the shortlisted sites is in the Pinelands [I].

## 5. Underwriting notes for the 6.5% YOC
- **Land basis:** Paramus traded at about $57k per approved 55+ unit (Bergen, closest in) [V/I]. Morris and Somerset campus land should be cheaper per unit [I].
- **Development cost:** CopperWood's AL CN cost is $57.18M for 96 AL units, about $596k/unit, including the reuse of an existing building [V/I]. Arbor Terrace is $22.1M for 100 beds [V]. Wide cost dispersion; test both.
- **Medicaid drag:** 10% of AL beds at Medicaid rates is a permanent revenue drag [V]. It is offset by the affordable credit value, which is often the price of town support [I].
- **Bed acquisition:** The SNF/continuum component needs purchased bed rights. Treat it as phase 2 [I].
